Sponsor a campaign moment, not a guaranteed finish.

An Appalachian Trail thru-hike is never guaranteed. Weather, injury, logistics, finances, health, or family obligations can change the trail plan. That is why sponsor opportunities are built around campaign moments that can still be delivered with integrity.

Sponsors support participation, progress, donor momentum, public updates, and transparent reporting — not a promise that every mile will go exactly as planned.

If the hike changes

The campaign still reports back

If the trail plan changes, the fundraiser can continue through honest updates, adjusted mileage reporting, continued donation links, sponsor recaps, and a final report showing what happened.

Community miles can expand the campaign.

The primary story is still Paul’s Appalachian Trail attempt. But if the pledge platform allows multiple participants, supporters may also be able to contribute their own miles through walks, hikes, runs, gym sessions, or business/team challenges.

Trail MilesMiles Paul personally completes on the Appalachian Trail.
Training MilesPre-hike and shakedown miles used to document preparation.
Community MilesOptional supporter miles that expand the fundraising challenge without replacing the A.T. attempt.

Simple rule: trail miles are mine; community miles are ours. Community participation can help build momentum, but it does not replace the original commitment to prepare seriously, attempt the hike honestly, raise money for CRI, and report what actually happened.

Supporter incentive ideas

A business can participate without a large upfront ad spend. Many incentives only cost money when a supporter actually buys.

Discount code

$5 off $30+, $10 off $50+, or 10% off with a max discount and expiration date.

Free item or upgrade

Free size upgrade, free small item with purchase, appetizer with entrée, or bonus service.

Supporter night

Drive traffic to a defined window, especially off-peak days or slower hours.

Bounce-back offer

Reward the next visit instead of discounting the first transaction.

Match unlock

Unlock a sponsor match after a certain number of donors, pledges, or QR scans.

Customer miles challenge

Let customers or staff log miles to unlock a donation or sponsor match.
