Supporter incentive ideas

A business can participate without a large upfront ad spend. Many incentives only cost money when a supporter actually buys.

Discount code

$5 off $30+, $10 off $50+, or 10% off with a max discount and expiration date.

Free item or upgrade

Free size upgrade, free small item with purchase, appetizer with entrée, or bonus service.

Supporter night

Drive traffic to a defined window, especially off-peak days or slower hours.

Bounce-back offer

Reward the next visit instead of discounting the first transaction.

Match unlock

Unlock a sponsor match after a certain number of donors, pledges, or QR scans.

Customer miles challenge

Let customers or staff log miles to unlock a donation or sponsor match.
