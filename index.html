Field Note 001 · Pre-Hike
The idea

Maine Is the Goal. Going the Distance Is the Point.

Going the Distance is built around the attempt rather than a finish-line guarantee. If the journey lasts 100 miles, those miles count. If it lasts 1,000, those miles count. If it reaches Katahdin, all of them count.

The fundraiser succeeds one completed mile at a time.

Transparency

The charitable path stays separate.

Per-mile pledges and one-time charitable donations are intended to be processed through the approved fundraising channel for the Cancer Research Institute. Going the Distance does not receive those charitable contributions.

Final CRI branding and payment implementation remain subject to confirmation before formal campaign launch.

Community partners

$2,200 for Cancer Research. $300 to Support the Campaign.

The working 2027 Founding Community Partner model is $2,500 total: a $2,200 guaranteed charitable commitment intended for CRI and a separate $300 Going the Distance campaign support contribution.

Operating principle

Raise more than we spend

Campaign support should help create more charitable value than it consumes.

The Appalachian Trail hike is personally funded. Partner money supports cancer research and the campaign infrastructure around it—not the personal cost of hiking the Trail.
