The concept

The hike creates the structure. The fundraiser creates the purpose. The content creates accountability. The reporting creates trust.

Direct donations

Supporters can give directly through an approved charity fundraising page once the final beneficiary and platform setup are confirmed.

Per-mile pledges

Supporters can pledge by the mile, capped by amount, milestone, or section. A dedicated pledge platform may handle the per-mile mechanics.

Sponsor matches

Businesses can sponsor sections, match donations, fund pledge caps, or offer supporter incentives that make the campaign more participatory.

Ways to support

Final donation links will be added after the charity and pledge-platform setup are approved. The intended structure keeps charitable donations separate from personal hike or content support.

What I will document

The content plan is designed to make the project followable without turning the hike into fake influencer theater.

Before the hike

  • Training and shakedown hikes
  • Gear tests and mistakes
  • Fundraising setup and sponsor outreach
  • Budget/logistics reality

On trail

  • Weekly or section recaps
  • Mileage and fundraising progress
  • Cause and sponsor updates
  • Honest notes on what worked and failed

Working timeline

PlanningConfirm charity setup, pledge platform, sponsor language, and campaign landing page.
Pre-hikeBuild donor list, secure sponsors, publish preparation content, test gear, and launch pledge pages.
On trailPost section recaps, donor updates, mileage progress, sponsor match pushes, and honest field notes.
AfterPublish final report: funds raised, miles completed, sponsor participation, lessons learned, and next steps.
