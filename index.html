How it works

A Few Cents. A Lot of Miles.

Pledge a few cents for every qualifying Appalachian Trail mile completed, or make a one-time donation instead.

01 / PLEDGE

Choose an amount.

Select a per-mile pledge and, if you want, set a maximum total contribution.

02 / FOLLOW

Follow the progress.

Qualifying Appalachian Trail mileage is logged as the hike moves north.

03 / CONTRIBUTE

The miles determine the gift.

After the hike ends, final qualifying mileage determines the pledge amount, subject to your cap.

1¢ / mile≈ $22 across a ~2,200-mile hike
2¢ / mile≈ $44 across a ~2,200-mile hike
5¢ / mile≈ $110 across a ~2,200-mile hike
10¢ / mile≈ $220 across a ~2,200-mile hike

Final contribution is based on actual qualifying mileage completed and any maximum amount selected.

Joining late? Your pledge still counts from Mile 1.
A pledge made after the hike begins applies to the total qualifying mileage completed during the campaign—including miles already completed before the pledge was made.

The idea

Maine Is the Goal. Going the Distance Is the Point.

There’s a difference between setting out to win something and setting out to find out whether you can keep going.

Going the Distance grew from that idea. The Appalachian Trail gives it a literal form: start in Georgia, point north, and keep walking.

The fundraiser succeeds one completed mile at a time.

If the journey lasts 100 miles, those miles count. If it lasts 1,000, those miles count. If it reaches Katahdin, all of them count. The attempt itself can do something useful.

Transparency

The charitable path stays separate.

Per-mile pledges and one-time charitable donations are intended to be processed through the approved fundraising channel for the Cancer Research Institute. Going the Distance does not receive those charitable contributions.

Final CRI branding and payment implementation remain subject to CRI confirmation before public launch.
