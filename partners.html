Business & Community Partnerships

Communities Going the Distance.

Your business can be the local connection to a much larger campaign.

Going the Distance gives local businesses a way to combine community engagement, meaningful visibility, and charitable participation without asking them to finance the personal Appalachian Trail adventure.

Founding Partner DetailsContact
The core principle

Sponsors fund the campaign around the adventure—not the adventure itself.

The Appalachian Trail attempt is personally funded. Sponsorship supports the public campaign: outreach, content, partner materials, community activation, website infrastructure, and other campaign-specific work.

Primary 2027 product

Founding Community Partner

$1,000

Activation commitment. No payment is due until the campaign passes its launch-ready gate.

What the partner gets

A real activation, not just a logo.

The offer is intentionally small enough to deliver well before and during the 2027 hike.

Visibility

Founding Partner recognition, website placement, partner profile, campaign social recognition, and post-hike acknowledgment.

Local participation

Partner-specific campaign QR materials give customers a direct way to pledge, donate, or learn about the project through the business.

Measurement

Basic referral/engagement tracking where practical, with a post-campaign summary of what the partnership helped generate.

Optional add-on

Match Activation

A Community Partner can separately amplify the charitable campaign by matching donations or pledges up to a defined amount.

Example

“For the next 100 trail miles, ABC Coffee will match donations to the Going the Distance cancer-research campaign up to $500.”

Separate money path

The business's charitable match goes through the approved CRI fundraising channel. It is not mixed with the commercial sponsorship payment.

Launch-ready gate

No sponsor payment while the project is still theoretical.

A business may reserve Founding Partner status before launch. The sponsorship only activates once the campaign is genuinely ready to go public.

Charitable relationship

CRI relationship, fundraising structure, and branding/payment implementation confirmed.

Fundraising system

RallyUp pledge, donation, cap, mileage, privacy, and mobile mechanics tested.

Public campaign home

Going the Distance website live and able to explain the hike, cause, and participation path.

Sponsor infrastructure

Partner page, recognition, QR/referral materials, and deliverable process ready.

Hike commitment

2027 start window established, major logistics in progress, and no known circumstance making the attempt unlikely to begin.

Business terms

Simple written agreement defines contribution, deliverables, timing, and what happens if plans change.

If plans change

The sponsor is not buying a guarantee of Katahdin.

  • If the campaign is canceled before meaningful sponsor benefits begin, the sponsorship payment is returned.
  • If the hike begins but ends early, the campaign continues around actual mileage and agreed deliverables.
  • If a promised deliverable becomes impossible, a comparable benefit or appropriate remedy is agreed with the sponsor.
Other support

Brand & Supporting Partners

Regional/national brand partnerships and in-kind support remain custom rather than becoming another pre-launch product catalog.

Useful categories could include footwear, packs, power/charging, food, lodging, transportation, printing, communications, or other services directly useful to the campaign.
