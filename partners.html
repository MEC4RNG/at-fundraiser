Communities Going the Distance.

A business can help fund the cause and help the campaign reach more people.

The working 2027 partner model separates the charitable commitment from the campaign support that helps activate it: $2,200 guaranteed for cancer research and $300 to help operate, promote, measure, maintain, and document Going the Distance.

The 2027 model

$2,500 total commitment. Two separate jobs.

The structure puts most of the commitment directly behind the charitable mission while reserving a small, clearly identified amount to support the campaign infrastructure and activation work that helps the match reach more people.

The $2,200 charitable commitment is deliberately close to the scale of the Appalachian Trail—roughly one dollar per mile. The $300 campaign support contribution helps keep the fundraising system operating and gives each matching commitment a meaningful activation.

$2,200 Guaranteed Commitment

Intended to move through the approved CRI fundraising path. During a featured matching window, eligible public donations may unlock the commitment dollar-for-dollar up to $2,200.

$300 Campaign Support

Supports partner activation, QR and print materials, content, outreach, promotion, website and domain costs, software, analytics, reporting, administration, and long-term campaign upkeep.

One Measurable Goal

Use campaign support to help generate more charitable value than the campaign resources consumed to produce it.

The Appalachian Trail hike remains personally funded. Neither component is intended to pay the personal cost of hiking the Trail.
How the money moves

Two separate paths. One partnership story.

The distinction matters for transparency: charitable support for CRI is not the same thing as non-charitable campaign support for Going the Distance.

Charitable commitment

Business → approved CRI fundraising path
The full $2,200 remains dedicated to CRI. Going the Distance does not receive this charitable money.

Campaign support

Business → Going the Distance
$300 supports the broader campaign, including the partner's featured activation. It is not represented as a charitable donation to CRI.

Featured matching partners

A fundraising moment, not just a logo.

Going the Distance plans to feature matching partners sequentially so each receives meaningful visibility and a distinct fundraising opportunity. Trail milestones may provide useful storytelling hooks, but no partner activation depends on reaching a particular mile or location.

Dedicated Match Window

One Featured Matching Partner is highlighted at a time under the campaign's match operating rules.

Permanent Recognition

Completed partners remain part of the 2027 campaign record rather than disappearing when the next match begins.

Measurement

Match utilization, referral activity, campaign costs, and attributable results are tracked where practical and summarized for the partner.

Launch-ready gate

No partner payment while the project is still theoretical.

A business may reserve Founding Partner status before launch. The partnership only activates once Going the Distance can responsibly go public and the CRI match structure is confirmed.

Business terms

Written agreement defines the $2,200 charitable commitment, $300 campaign support contribution, featured activation, timing, and contingency rules.

If plans change

The partner is not buying a guarantee of Katahdin.

  • If the campaign is canceled before meaningful activation begins, treatment of the $300 campaign support contribution is governed by the written agreement.
  • The $2,200 charitable commitment follows the approved CRI terms and never becomes campaign operating money.
  • If the hike begins but ends early, donor pledges and campaign reporting use actual qualifying mileage completed.
  • A Featured Matching Partner activation can continue around the actual campaign outcome rather than depending on a particular Trail milestone.
Limited capacity

Meaningful partnerships require limits.

Going the Distance intends to limit the number of Featured Matching Partners so each company receives a real activation rather than diluted logo placement. Additional businesses may be waitlisted, support CRI directly, or explore useful in-kind support.

$2,200 for cancer research. $300 to support the campaign that puts it to work.

A deliberately simple partnership designed to keep the charitable component larger than the campaign-support component.
