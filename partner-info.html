2027 Founding Community Partner

$2,500. Two Separate Commitments.

$2,200 reserved to match cancer-research support. $300 to activate the campaign around it.

The partnership is designed so the charitable commitment is substantially larger than the campaign expense—and so the $300 has a defined job: help attract additional participation rather than simply fund general overhead.

Total partner commitment
$2,500

$2,200 CRI matching commitment
$300 Going the Distance activation

88% match / 12% activation. Two separate money paths. Final match administration remains subject to CRI approval.

Why this structure

Use a small campaign investment to leverage a larger charitable result.

The $2,200 figure mirrors the approximate length of the Appalachian Trail: roughly one matching dollar for every mile. The objective is for public donors to unlock the matching pool while the campaign activation helps more people encounter and understand the opportunity.

The split

What each dollar is for.

$2,200 — Cancer Research Match

Intended to move through the approved Cancer Research Institute fundraising path, not through Going the Distance. The match can be structured as an “up to $2,200” pool that is unlocked by public giving, subject to CRI's preferred process.

  • Direct charitable purpose
  • Public matching story
  • Potential to stimulate additional gifts
  • Separate from campaign operating funds

$300 — Campaign Activation

A small commercial/community-partnership contribution used only for campaign execution and partner deliverables.

  • Partner-specific QR and print materials
  • Campaign creative/content production
  • Local activation and outreach
  • Referral/engagement tracking where practical
  • Other defined incremental promotion costs
The $300 activation payment is not represented as a charitable donation. It supports Going the Distance campaign execution. The $2,200 match is the charitable component and remains separate.
Money flow

Two paths. No muddle.

Charitable match

Business → approved CRI fundraising channel
Up to $2,200 is reserved to match eligible public donations or pledges according to CRI-approved mechanics.

Campaign activation

Business → Going the Distance
$300 supports agreed campaign work and partner benefits. It does not pay the personal cost of the Appalachian Trail hike.

Included activation

Founding Partner deliverables.

The activation package is designed to help the match generate attention and participation rather than simply providing passive logo placement.

  • Permanent 2027 Founding Community Partner recognition
  • Logo and website link in the campaign partner section
  • Dedicated partner profile/blurb
  • One dedicated partner spotlight on campaign social channels
  • Digital and printable campaign/match QR materials
  • Permission to use Going the Distance Community Partner badge
  • Periodic partner-recognition and match content
  • Post-campaign thank-you / impact summary and basic referral metrics where practical
Success standard

The activation should earn its keep.

The campaign's financial goal is not merely to spend money attractively. Campaign spending should help generate more charitable value than it consumes.

  • Track campaign activation expenses
  • Track donations and attributable pledge value where practical
  • Distinguish realized donations from projected per-mile pledges
  • Stop or revise promotional channels that do not justify their cost
A.T. commitment

The hike is personally funded.

The partner is supporting the public fundraising campaign, not financing a personal backpacking trip. Completion of the full Appalachian Trail is the goal, not a guarantee or condition of the partnership.

Best fit

Community-minded and outdoor-aligned businesses.

Particularly strong prospects include outdoor brands, A.T.-corridor businesses, hospitality and retail businesses, and companies with an established history of charitable giving or community sponsorship.

A clear boundary

No implied CRI endorsement.

A Going the Distance partnership does not imply that CRI sponsors, endorses, or has approved the participating business unless CRI separately authorizes such a relationship.

Start with a conversation

Reserve first. Activate when launch-ready.

A prospective partner can express interest while the campaign finishes its CRI implementation and launch gates. The $2,200 match and $300 activation should not be collected until their respective money paths and written terms are ready.
