2027 Founding Community Partner

A Simple Local Partnership.

Make your community-marketing dollars work twice: for your business and for a cause people can participate in.

The primary 2027 business product is intentionally simple: one defined partnership with practical deliverables, plus an optional charitable matching activation.

Activation commitment
$1,000

No payment is due until Going the Distance passes the launch-ready gate and can begin delivering partner value.

What it is

Your business becomes a local doorway into the campaign.

Customers encounter Going the Distance through a business they already know, while your company receives recognition, campaign content, community association, and measurable participation where practical.

Included

Founding Partner deliverables.

  • Permanent 2027 Founding Community Partner recognition
  • Logo and website link in the campaign partner section
  • Dedicated partner profile/blurb
  • One dedicated partner spotlight on campaign social channels
  • Digital and printable QR campaign materials
  • Permission to use Going the Distance Community Partner badge
  • Periodic partner-recognition content
  • Post-hike sponsor thank-you / impact summary and basic referral metrics where practical
Money flow

Three paths. No muddle.

Sponsorship

Business → Going the Distance
Supports campaign operations and partner deliverables.

Charitable gifts

Donor → RallyUp → CRI
Per-mile pledges and one-time donations remain in the charitable path.

Optional match

Business → approved CRI campaign
A separate match can amplify donor participation.

Best fit

Community-facing businesses.

Restaurants, coffee shops, gyms, banks, dealerships, local retailers, professional services, hospitality businesses, and other organizations with a genuine local audience.

A clear boundary

No implied CRI endorsement.

A Going the Distance sponsorship is separate from a charitable donation to CRI and does not imply that CRI sponsors, endorses, or has approved the participating business unless CRI separately authorizes such a relationship.

Start with a conversation

Reserve first. Activate when launch-ready.

A prospective partner can express interest and reserve Founding Partner status while the campaign finishes its launch gates. No money needs to move while the project is still being assembled.
