2027 Founding Community Partner

$2,500. Two Separate Commitments.

$2,200 guaranteed for cancer research. $300 to support the campaign that puts the match to work.

The partnership keeps the charitable commitment substantially larger than the campaign-support component. The two amounts remain financially distinct, and the Appalachian Trail hike itself remains personally funded.

Total partner commitment
$2,500

$2,200 guaranteed CRI charitable commitment
$300 Going the Distance campaign support contribution

88% charitable commitment / 12% campaign support. Final matching mechanics remain subject to CRI confirmation.

Why this structure

Use a small campaign investment to leverage a larger charitable result.

The $2,200 figure mirrors the approximate length of the Appalachian Trail: roughly one charitable dollar for every mile. The current plan is to use partner commitments as featured matching challenges that encourage additional public giving, while the full $2,200 remains committed to CRI even if a matching window is not fully utilized.

The split

What each commitment is for.

$2,200 — Guaranteed Cancer Research Commitment

Intended to move through the approved Cancer Research Institute fundraising path, not through Going the Distance. Eligible public gifts may unlock the commitment dollar-for-dollar during the partner's featured matching window, subject to CRI's preferred process.

  • Guaranteed charitable purpose
  • Public matching story
  • Potential to stimulate additional gifts
  • Separate from campaign operating funds

$300 — Campaign Support Contribution

A separate non-charitable contribution to Going the Distance that supports the operation, promotion, measurement, maintenance, and long-term documentation of the campaign.

  • Partner-specific QR and print materials
  • Campaign creative and content production
  • Promotion, outreach, and local activation
  • Website, domain, software, analytics, and tracking
  • Reporting, administration, and campaign upkeep
The $300 campaign support contribution is not represented as a charitable donation to CRI. It supports Going the Distance as a whole and is not limited solely to expenses incurred during one partner's matching window. The $2,200 charitable commitment remains separate.
Money flow

Two paths. No muddle.

Charitable commitment

Business → approved CRI fundraising channel
The full $2,200 is intended for CRI. During the partner's featured match, eligible public donations may be matched dollar-for-dollar up to $2,200 under CRI-approved mechanics.

Campaign support

Business → Going the Distance
$300 supports campaign infrastructure, promotion, measurement, partner activation, reporting, maintenance, and related campaign work. It does not pay the personal cost of the Appalachian Trail hike.

Featured matching activation

A real fundraising moment, not just a logo.

Going the Distance plans to feature matching partners sequentially so each company can receive a meaningful activation rather than disappearing into a wall of logos. Trail milestones may enrich the storytelling, but a partner's activation is not contingent on reaching a specific mile, town, state, or finish line.

  • Dedicated Featured Matching Partner window
  • Permanent 2027 Founding Community Partner recognition
  • Logo and website link in the campaign partner section
  • Dedicated partner profile and match-launch content
  • Digital and printable campaign/match QR materials
  • Match-progress and completion recognition
  • Referral and engagement tracking where practical
  • Post-activation and post-campaign impact reporting
Success standard

The campaign support should earn its keep.

The campaign's financial goal is not merely to spend money attractively. Campaign support should help generate more charitable value than it consumes.

  • Track campaign-support expenses separately from charitable funds
  • Track realized donations and match utilization where practical
  • Distinguish realized donations from projected per-mile pledges
  • Stop or revise promotional channels that do not justify their cost
  • Maintain campaign-level accounting of how partner support was used
A.T. commitment

The hike is personally funded.

The partner is supporting the public fundraising campaign, not financing a personal backpacking trip. Completion of the full Appalachian Trail is the goal, not a guarantee or condition of the partnership.

Best fit

Community-minded and outdoor-aligned businesses.

Particularly strong prospects include outdoor brands, A.T.-corridor businesses, hospitality and retail businesses, and companies with an established history of charitable giving or community sponsorship.

A clear boundary

No implied CRI endorsement.

A Going the Distance partnership does not imply that CRI sponsors, endorses, or has approved the participating business unless CRI separately authorizes such a relationship.

Start with a conversation

Reserve first. Activate when launch-ready.

A prospective partner can express interest while the campaign finishes its CRI implementation and launch gates. Featured partner capacity will be intentionally limited so each activation can be executed meaningfully. The $2,200 charitable commitment and $300 campaign support contribution should not be collected until their respective money paths and written terms are ready.
