Pre-Launch Disclosures

Clear Boundaries.

This pre-launch website is intentionally explicit about what is confirmed, what is still pending, and which parts of the campaign remain separate from one another.

Pre-launch status

This website is publicly accessible as a campaign-development and pre-launch site. It is not yet the final public fundraising implementation. The CRI relationship, charitable payment path, matching mechanics, receipt language, branding permissions, privacy policy, and final partnership agreement remain subject to confirmation before formal campaign launch.

Charitable donations and per-mile pledges

Going the Distance is designed so charitable donations and per-mile pledges intended for the Cancer Research Institute are processed through an approved fundraising channel rather than being received by the campaign operator.

Founding Community Partner structure

The current working 2027 Founding Community Partner model is a $2,500 total commitment divided into two separate components: a $2,200 guaranteed charitable commitment intended for CRI and a $300 Going the Distance campaign support contribution. The two components are not interchangeable and are not represented as a single charitable donation.

$2,200 charitable matching component

The intended structure is for the business's $2,200 charitable commitment to move through an approved CRI fundraising path. Going the Distance should not receive or repurpose this money as campaign operating funds. The campaign intends to use partner commitments as sequential featured matching challenges, while the full $2,200 remains committed to CRI even if a matching window is not fully utilized. Exact timing, eligibility, administration, and public wording remain subject to CRI confirmation.

$300 campaign support component

The $300 campaign support contribution is a separate non-charitable payment to Going the Distance. It may support partner activation, QR and print materials, campaign creative, outreach, promotion, website and domain costs, software, analytics, tracking, reporting, administration, post-campaign maintenance, and long-term campaign documentation. It is not represented as a charitable contribution to CRI and is not limited solely to expenses incurred during one partner's matching window.

Unused campaign support

Once a partnership has meaningfully activated, the $300 campaign support contribution supports the broader Going the Distance campaign rather than functioning as an expense-by-expense reimbursement account. If the campaign is canceled before meaningful activation or partner benefits begin, treatment of the contribution will be governed by the final written partnership agreement.

Campaign efficiency

Going the Distance intends to track campaign-support costs separately from charitable funds generated. The operating standard is that campaign spending should help generate more charitable value than it consumes. Projected per-mile pledge value should be distinguished from charitable dollars already received.

Featured matching partners

The campaign intends to feature matching partners sequentially rather than activate multiple partner matches against the same public donation. Trail milestones may be used for storytelling, but a partner's featured activation is not intended to depend on reaching a particular mile, state, town, or completion point.

Business endorsement

A Going the Distance partnership does not imply that CRI sponsors, endorses, or has approved the participating business unless CRI separately authorizes such a relationship.

Personal hike costs

The Appalachian Trail attempt is personally funded. Neither business charitable commitments nor campaign-support contributions are intended to cover the personal costs of backpacking the Appalachian Trail.

Hike completion

The Appalachian Trail attempt involves uncertainty. No donor or partner is promised that the entire Trail will be completed. Per-mile pledges are based on qualifying mileage actually completed.

Trail information

Public route and progress information on this website is campaign storytelling, not a navigation guide, emergency-locator service, or substitute for current Appalachian Trail Conservancy, National Park Service, land-manager, weather, or safety information.

2027 mileage

The website currently uses the official 2026 Appalachian Trail length of 2,197.9 miles as a planning reference. It will be updated when the official 2027 mileage is published.

Privacy

The production fundraising website should publish a final privacy notice describing any analytics, email subscription, cookies, forms, and partner referral tracking actually used. The current static site does not directly collect personal information.

Legal and tax language

Nothing on this pre-launch website should be interpreted as legal, accounting, or tax advice. Final partner agreements, charitable receipt language, matching terms, and tax statements should be reviewed by the appropriate organizations or professionals before formal campaign launch.
