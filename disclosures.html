Pre-Launch Disclosures

Clear Boundaries.

This prototype is intentionally explicit about what is confirmed, what is still pending, and which parts of the campaign are separate from one another.

Private pre-launch status

This website is still a pre-launch prototype. It should not be treated as the final public fundraising page until the CRI relationship, payment path, matching mechanics, receipt language, branding permissions, privacy policy, and partnership agreement are finalized.

Charitable donations and per-mile pledges

Going the Distance is designed so that charitable donations and per-mile pledges intended for the Cancer Research Institute are processed through an approved fundraising channel rather than being received by the campaign operator.

Founding Community Partner structure

The proposed 2027 Founding Community Partner commitment is $2,500 total, divided into two separate components: $2,200 intended for a CRI matching pool and $300 intended for Going the Distance campaign activation. The two components are not interchangeable and should not be represented as a single charitable donation.

$2,200 charitable matching component

The intended structure is for the business's charitable matching commitment to move through an approved CRI fundraising path. Going the Distance should not receive or repurpose this money as campaign operating funds. The exact timing, eligibility, charging, and public wording of the match remain subject to CRI approval.

$300 campaign activation component

The $300 activation contribution is a separate commercial/community-partnership payment to Going the Distance for defined campaign execution and partner deliverables. It may support items such as QR and print materials, partner-specific creative, local activation, outreach, tracking, and other incremental promotional costs. It is not represented as a tax-deductible charitable contribution to CRI.

Campaign efficiency

Going the Distance intends to track campaign operating costs separately from charitable funds generated. The internal success standard is that campaign spending should help generate more charitable value than it consumes. Projected per-mile pledge value should be distinguished from charitable dollars already received.

Business endorsement

A Going the Distance partnership does not imply that CRI sponsors, endorses, or has approved the participating business unless CRI separately authorizes such a relationship.

Personal hike costs

The Appalachian Trail attempt is personally funded. Neither business matching commitments nor campaign-activation payments are intended to cover the personal costs of backpacking the Appalachian Trail.

Hike completion

The Appalachian Trail attempt involves uncertainty. No donor or partner is promised that the entire Trail will be completed. Per-mile pledges are based on qualifying mileage actually completed.

Trail information

Public route and progress information on this website is campaign storytelling, not a navigation guide, emergency-locator service, or substitute for current Appalachian Trail Conservancy, National Park Service, land-manager, weather, or safety information.

2027 mileage

The website currently uses the official 2026 Appalachian Trail length of 2,197.9 miles as a planning reference. It will be updated when the official 2027 mileage is published.

Privacy

The production website should publish a final privacy notice describing any analytics, email subscription, cookies, forms, and partner referral tracking actually used. This static prototype does not collect personal information.

Legal and tax language

Nothing in this prototype should be interpreted as legal, accounting, or tax advice. Final partner agreements, charitable receipt language, matching terms, and tax statements should be reviewed by the appropriate organizations or professionals before public use.
