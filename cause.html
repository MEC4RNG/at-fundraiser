The Cause

Making the Miles Useful.

A personal challenge can still produce something larger than itself.

Going the Distance is being developed to turn verified Appalachian Trail progress into support for cancer research, with the Cancer Research Institute as the intended charitable beneficiary.

Why research

Progress is cumulative.

Cancer research is built the same way this campaign is: step by step, experiment by experiment, mile by mile. The immediate outcome is not always visible, but meaningful progress is made by continuing to push the boundary of what is known and what can be treated.

Cancer Research Institute

A science-first organization focused on cancer immunotherapy.

CRI describes its mission as creating a world immune to cancer by advancing scientific discovery, accelerating collaboration, and turning breakthroughs into treatments. Its work focuses on cancer immunotherapy—the use of the immune system to prevent, control, and eliminate cancer.

Public use of CRI name, logo, donation wording, and campaign relationship language will follow CRI's final approval and implementation guidance.

The fundraising model

Every completed mile can matter.

The campaign does not require a successful 2,200-mile finish for a pledge to have value.

Per-mile pledge

Choose a cents-per-mile amount. The final gift is based on qualifying mileage completed, subject to any donor-set maximum.

One-time gift

Supporters who prefer a normal donation can give immediately rather than waiting for the hike to conclude.

Late pledge

A pledge made mid-hike applies retroactively to all qualifying campaign mileage, including miles completed before the pledge was made.

Transparency rules

Simple rules create trust.

  • Going the Distance does not receive charitable donations intended for CRI.
  • Campaign sponsorship is a separate commercial/community partnership path.
  • The personal cost of the Appalachian Trail hike is not dependent on sponsor funding.
  • Donors may set a maximum on per-mile pledges.
  • Qualifying mileage is defined before launch.
  • RallyUp activity uses completed whole miles, rounded down.
  • Projected/pledged support is distinguished from donations already collected.
  • CRI's final public wording and branding will be used once approved.
